### Event Objectives

- Understand product development strategy (POC → MVP) and infrastructure deployment on AWS Cloud
- Grasp Voice AI architecture (Speech-to-Speech or STT → LLM → TTS) and the challenges of deploying it for Vietnamese.
- Learn about AI Agents (DevOps Agent, MCP) for automating incident handling and reducing Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR).
- Explore AI applications (Amazon Q) in recruitment such as: CV screening, candidate scoring, and interview process optimization.
- Understand how to establish secure connections between AI and internal enterprise data through MCP Server and private VPC.

## Main Content of the Workshop

### 1. AgenticOps For Your Cloud (System Operations and Startup Strategy)

**Speaker:** Steve Trần  
**Position:** CTO/Founder, Cloud Thinker

#### **Main Content**

- Shared the process of migrating infrastructure from traditional on-premise servers to AWS Cloud. Mr. Steve Trần emphasized an execution mindset, the importance of building products along a path from POC (Proof of Concept) to MVP (Minimum Viable Product), as well as how to connect with champion customers such as F88 or FPT to solve real business problems.

#### **Knowledge Gained**

- Understand a practical approach to migrating operational infrastructure to the Cloud in a startup context.
- Grasp the product development roadmap from POC to MVP.
- Learn from experience in finding and collaborating with champion customers to solve real business problems.

---

### 2. Building Voice Agent at Scale (Voice AI in the Enterprise)

**Speaker:** Doanh Hoàng Hiếu Nghị, Kiệt Trần, Trung Vũ  
**Position:** AI Engineer, Renova Cloud; AI Engineer, AWS Student Builder Group; CEO, Revve AI

#### **Main Content**

- Introduced common architectures for building Voice AI, from direct Speech-to-Speech models to the 3-component model (Speech-to-Text → LLM → Text-to-Speech). The speakers focused on analyzing deployment challenges in the Vietnamese market: scarce speech data (low-resource language), difficulties in gender and regional accent recognition, along with context management techniques to avoid user interruption errors (interruption handling). Mr. Hiếu Nghị led the coordination and model architecture portion; Mr. Kiệt shared real-world product perspectives and technical demos; Mr. Trung V. contributed in depth on LLM and Speech-to-Text techniques.

#### **Knowledge Gained**

- Grasp common architectures (Speech-to-Speech, or STT-LLM-TTS) for building a Voice Agent.
- Understand technical barriers specific to applying Voice AI for the Vietnamese market and language (scarce data, regional accents, gender).
- Learn about conversational context management techniques to handle interruption scenarios and improve user experience.

---

### 3. AWS DevOps Agent Your Always Available Operations Teammate (Automation with DevOps Agent)

**Speaker:** Bao Phan, Nguyen Nguyen  
**Position:** Cloud Engineer – Cloud Kinetics

#### **Main Content**

- Presented how to automate incident investigation processes using DevOps Agent, including steps such as: log classification, root cause investigation, remediation recommendations, and system improvement. A key highlight was applying Model Context Protocol (MCP) to connect AI Agents with operational systems, significantly optimizing Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR). Ms. Bao was the main speaker on automated incident investigation processes; Mr. Nguyen supported with in-depth presentation on system connectivity via MCP.

#### **Knowledge Gained**

- Understand the automated incident investigation process: log classification, root cause investigation, remediation recommendations, and system improvement.
- Grasp the concept and role of Model Context Protocol (MCP) in connecting AI Agents with operational systems.
- Recognize the benefits of reducing MTTR for system stability and reliability.

---

### 4. AI – Powered Productivity Workforce Planning For Enterprise (AI and Workforce Management)

**Speaker:** Truong Tran, Anh Dang  
**Position:** AI Solution Sales, Noventiq

#### **Main Content**

- Introduced a solution applying Amazon Q to support Human Resources (HR): automating CV screening, analyzing candidate data based on Job Description (JD), scoring candidates, and optimizing the interview process. The session emphasized increasing HR productivity as well as the security of internal data. Mr. Trường presented the Amazon Q solution and system security techniques; Ms. Minh Anh shared real-world HR challenges in the AI era.

#### **Knowledge Gained**

- Understand how to apply Amazon Q to specific HR functions: CV screening, candidate analysis, and scoring based on JD.
- Recognize the potential for increased productivity and more accurate decision-making in recruitment through AI.
- Grasp the importance of ensuring internal data security when applying AI to HR operations.

---

### 5. Building Secure Private MCP for Quick (Security & System Connectivity)

**Speaker:** Toan Nguyen, Truong Tran  
**Position:** AI Solution Sales, Noventiq; AWS Security Builder

#### **Main Content**

- Delved deeper into the technique of setting up an MCP server to connect Amazon Q with third-party data sources privately, without going through the public Internet, ensuring security in the enterprise's Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) environment.

#### **Knowledge Gained**

- Understand the technique of setting up an MCP server to securely connect AI with third-party data sources.
- Learn how to deploy private connectivity in a VPC environment without going through the public Internet.
- Gain a clearer awareness of the importance of security when integrating AI into internal enterprise data systems.
